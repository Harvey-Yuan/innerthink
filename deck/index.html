InnerThink

Reasoning that never becomes words.

What it is
6 continuous thoughtsThe chain of thought stays in the residual stream as six latent vectors. Nothing is decoded to text.
How it's built
Self-distillationOne network, two branches. The verbal branch teaches the latent branch by matching a single hidden state.
What it costs
A rank-128 adapterBase weights frozen. The reasoning behaviour ships as a LoRA plus one projection MLP.
GSM8K · MATH-500 · BBH latent 49.3% / explicit 49.9% 2.7–5.9× faster decode 3.1× reasoning compression
Mechanism

The model runs six forward passes
that no one ever reads.

Each thought is the previous hidden state, projected and fed back in as the next input embedding. Training teaches those vectors to carry what the sentences used to.

Architecture after Shen et al., CODI: Compressing Chain-of-Thought into Continuous Space via Self-Distillation, EMNLP 2025.
